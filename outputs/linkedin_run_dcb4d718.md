🔒 **Cybersecurity Attacks: What You Need to Know**

A cybersecurity attack is any attempt to gain unauthorized access to digital systems. The most common attack types include:

- **Phishing**  
- **Malware**  
- **Ransomware**  
- **Denial‑of‑Service (DoS)**  
- **Password attacks**  
- **Data breaches**

These threats can target anyone—from individuals to businesses, schools, banks, hospitals, and government organizations. The impact can be severe:

- Theft of personal or confidential information  
- Financial losses  
- Disruption of essential services  
- Damage to an organization’s reputation  

**Preventive measures** that help safeguard against these risks:

- Use strong, unique passwords  
- Enable multi‑factor authentication  
- Deploy firewalls and antivirus software  
- Keep systems and software updated regularly  
- Maintain secure data backups  
- Educate employees and users about suspicious emails, links, and websites  
- Detect, isolate, investigate, and prevent future attacks promptly  

Staying vigilant and implementing these best practices is essential for protecting data, finances, and trust in today’s digital landscape.
