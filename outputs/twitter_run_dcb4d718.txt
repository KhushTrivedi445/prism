Cyber threats are on the rise—phishing, malware, ransomware, DoS, password attacks, and data breaches target individuals, businesses, schools, banks, hospitals, and government agencies. Consequences include data theft, financial loss, service disruption, and reputational damage. Protect yourself with strong passwords, MFA, firewalls, antivirus, regular updates, secure backups, and user education. #Cybersecurity #DataProtection
